## 1 Purpose

Synthetic training standard for mandatory human review of all
recommendations produced by the investigation assistant.

## 2 Advisory output only

Assistant output is advisory. A recommendation is never a final decision
and never authorises an operational step such as blocking a card, issuing
a refund, modifying an account or contacting a customer.

## 3 Reviewer responsibility

An authorised reviewer owns the final disposition, verifies the cited
evidence and may accept, amend or reject the recommendation.

## 4 Mandatory rationale

Every recommendation must carry a concise factual rationale and explicit
missing information so the reviewer can audit the reasoning path without
hidden chain-of-thought.

## 5 Escalation conditions

Escalate to a senior reviewer when evidence is contradictory, when policy
guidance is unavailable, or when the recommendation carries low confidence.
