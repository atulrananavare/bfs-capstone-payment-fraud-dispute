## 1 Purpose

Synthetic training guidance for disputes where the customer recognises the
merchant but disputes some aspect of the transaction.

## 2 Recognition test

The dispute-operations route applies only when the merchant is recognised by
the customer. If the merchant is not recognised, treat the case under
unauthorised-transaction handling instead.

## 3 Complaint categories

Covered complaint types: goods or services not received (non-receipt),
service cancelled but charged (cancellation), and amount discrepancies
between the agreed and posted amounts.

## 4 Dispute-operations routing

When the merchant is recognised and the complaint is a fulfilment,
cancellation or amount issue, route the case to dispute operations for
chargeback evaluation. Fraud investigation routing is not appropriate for a
recognised merchant service complaint.

## 5 Evidence requirements

Support the recommendation with the customer intake claim, the transaction
record and any merchant risk signal that affects the dispute evaluation.
Record missing evidence explicitly rather than assuming it.
